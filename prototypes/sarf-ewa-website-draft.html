Why Sarf

Lending should not cost you
more than you borrowed.

EarnIn and Dave provide early wage access, but they do not provide an additional 0% bridge loan. SoFi and Upstart offer personal loans but charge interest. Sarf combines earned wage access with an additional 0% bridge loan, using verified payroll information instead of a credit score.

How it works

From signup to cash in three steps.

Step 01
Connect your payroll
Link your employer through our payroll integration. We verify your income, not your credit score. It takes about 60 seconds.
Step 02
Get up to $1,500 at 0%
Access up to $700 as a 0% bridge loan, plus your earned wages, up to $1,500 in total. No interest and no origination fee. Your assessment fee is shown clearly before you continue.
Step 03
Repay automatically
Your earned-wage access is deducted from your next paycheck. Any bridge loan is divided into three automatic payments beginning with the following paycheck.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sarf earned wage access and the additional 0% bridge loan.

Sarf connects to your payroll provider and verifies your employer, income and pay history directly. Because repayment is scheduled against your paychecks through that connection, Sarf does not price the loan with interest. The only stated charge is a flat Sarf Financial Assessment fee, shown to you before you continue.
A flat fee covers the Sarf Financial Assessment. Sarf verifies your employer, income, cash flow, and employment history through your payroll connection. It is separate from the amount you access, and your bridge loan is always 0% interest. Your first assessment is $10. After that it is $20, or $35 on later assessments when you request $700 or more.
Eligible users may access up to $1,500 in total, based on verified income and earned wages. The amount may be entirely earned wage access, or a combination of earned wages and an additional 0% bridge loan. The bridge-loan portion is limited to $700. All amounts remain subject to eligibility and approval.
Your earned-wage access is deducted automatically from your next paycheck. Any bridge loan is divided into three equal payments. The first begins with the paycheck after the one your earned-wage access came out of, and the remaining two follow on the next applicable paychecks.
No. Sarf does not use a credit pull to determine eligibility. Qualification is based on verified employment, income, and payroll information. Eligibility is not guaranteed.
Contact Sarf support as soon as possible. Available repayment options are governed by your agreement and Sarf’s applicable support policies.
Sarf connects to over 1,500 payroll providers, covering the vast majority of employed workers in the US. If yours isn’t supported, you won’t be able to proceed at this time, but we’re expanding coverage regularly.
