Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sarf earned wage access.

Sarf gives you access to a portion of the wages you've already earned during your current pay period, ahead of your scheduled payday.
You connect your payroll account so Sarf can see how much you've earned so far this pay period. Based on that, you're offered an amount you may be eligible to access now, repaid in one deduction on your next payday.
Active employment and a supported payroll connection are required. Eligibility and the amount available are based on your verified payroll information.
Sarf connects to your payroll provider using Pinwheel to verify your income and, if you proceed, to set up repayment from your paycheck.
No. Sarf does not run a credit check or require a FICO score. Eligibility is based on your verified payroll information.
The amount available is based on how much of your current pay period you've already earned, up to a program maximum. Final amounts are subject to approval.
A flat fee that covers verifying your employer and income. It's shown to you before you accept, and does not change based on how much you access.
The amount you accessed is deducted in one transaction from your next paycheck through your connected payroll account.
Approved funds are loaded onto your Sarf card, subject to operational confirmation.
